# Twang ELI5 image-generation prompts

Mode: built-in `imagegen` tool. The built-in tool does not expose a model selector, so the exact underlying model is not recorded here.

All images use a shared visual direction: premium modern children's science-book editorial illustration, warm cream paper, gouache texture plus clean digital shapes, deep charcoal outlines, warm yellow, coral, mint, sky blue, no text, no logo, no watermark, no blood or medical horror.

## hero.webp

Wide website hero with generous negative space on the left. Show a friendly side-profile cutaway of a human head and neck. Tiny vocal folds make a sound pulse; the vocal tract opens like a megaphone; bright sound waves leave the mouth. The sound must originate at the larynx, not the nose. Simplified but plausible anatomy.

## source-filter-ear.webp

Wide left-to-right educational triptych: air from lungs makes vocal folds pulse and creates many harmonics; the waves travel through a vocal-tract filter that boosts selected bands; the brighter result reaches a friendly listener's ear. Show cause and effect without written labels or a magical single frequency.

## anatomy.webp

Centered sagittal head-and-neck cutaway. Distinguish the true vocal folds, short epilaryngeal tube, aryepiglottic entrance region, oropharynx, open mouth, raised soft palate and closed nasal port. Show the path widening toward the mouth. Do not confuse false vocal folds with AES; no single magic muscle.

## nasal-vs-twang.webp

Matched side-by-side cutaways of one person. Left: cool blue sound enters an open nasal passage and exits partly through the nose. Right: the soft palate closes the nasal passage and warm sound travels through a somewhat narrowed throat and open mouth. Both sounds originate at the vocal folds; oral twang does not require nasal opening.

## research-methods.webp

Friendly voice-science lab collage around a singer: MRI head scan, laryngoscope view, acoustic spectrum, airflow/pressure apparatus and a small listening panel. Visually suggest small samples and uncertainty. Make clear that different methods provide different evidence and no single method proves everything.

## practice.webp

Friendly vocal coach and adult student exploring a bright, clear voice with relaxed posture and comfortable mouth opening. Include small vignettes for transferring the cue into a sung phrase and stopping when the whole throat is painfully tense. Sensory cues are exploration tools, not anatomical proof.

## twang-belt-opera.webp

Three diverse adult singers in tasteful cutaway profile: bright twang-like sound, strong speech-like belt, and classical ring with a wider pharynx/darker vowel. Mark the same upper-larynx entrance region subtly in all three to show a shared component while keeping the overall sounds and tract shapes different. Avoid genre stereotypes and fixed universal anatomy.
