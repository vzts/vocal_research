# 뮤지션과 성공 시각 가이드 이미지 프롬프트

모든 이미지는 OpenAI `imagegen`으로 생성했다. 그림은 개념 이해를 위한 생성 모식도이며 실제 인물, 통계 그래프, 사건 사진 또는 사업 성과의 재현이 아니다. 이미지 안에 글자·숫자·로고를 넣지 않았다.

## `hero.webp`

```text
Create a wide 16:9 editorial illustration for a Korean beginner-friendly research guide about what success means for musicians. Show a diverse group of entirely fictional musicians at a branching landscape: one path reaches a lively small community venue, another a quiet composition studio with a growing catalog, another a large stage, another a sustainable home-and-work life. Above them floats a beautiful multi-faceted compass made of six translucent shapes representing craft, audience, livelihood, autonomy, longevity, and wellbeing, but with absolutely no text, numbers, icons, labels, logos, charts, or watermarks. Make it clear there is no single finish line. Sophisticated screen-print and cut-paper collage, cobalt blue, coral, saffron, mint, warm ivory, charcoal, rich tactile grain, bold silhouettes, strong negative space on the left for HTML headline overlay, accessible high contrast, warm and humane, not corporate, no real celebrities, no photorealistic faces, no fake UI.
```

## `market-uncertainty.webp`

```text
Create a wide 16:9 conceptual editorial illustration explaining unpredictability and cumulative social influence in music markets without using any text or data. At the bottom, several equally polished glowing music seeds begin side by side. As they move upward through a branching pinball-like field of audience clusters, recommendation currents, timing gates, media spotlights, and random encounters, their paths diverge dramatically; one becomes highly visible, several find medium communities, and others remain nearly unseen. The quality of the seeds should look comparable so visibility is not mistaken for intrinsic worth. Screen-print plus paper-cut collage, cobalt, coral, saffron, mint, ivory and charcoal, tactile grain, clean readable composition, no numbers, no axes, no labels, no logos, no real platforms, no celebrity likenesses, no fake statistics.
```

## `case-mechanisms.webp`

```text
Create a wide 16:9 educational montage of five fictional musician case mechanisms, with no portraits of real artists and no text. Show: a songwriter carefully making a new owned recording beside an older locked archive; an established band opening a flexible-price digital doorway to a large waiting audience; an independent performer supported by many small patron hands while also managing boxes and fulfillment; a self-managed punk band running an all-ages community show with low-cost simple infrastructure; and a touring duo studying a ledger where a full venue still leaves an empty fuel tank. Visually connect each scene to a different lesson—rights, accumulated audience trust, gross versus cost, constraint-based autonomy, and revenue versus profit. Editorial cut-paper screen-print, cobalt, coral, saffron, mint, ivory, charcoal, tactile grain, clear panel rhythm but no borders containing text, no numbers, labels, logos, brands, celebrity likenesses, or recognizable album art.
```

```text
Create a square 1:1 conceptual illustration showing musician success as a balanced dashboard rather than a ladder. Different vessels are intentionally filled to different levels and connected by flexible cords, showing tradeoffs and personal priorities; none is universally maximized. No text, letters, numbers, axes, labels, logos, currency symbols, medals, trophies, or charts. Refined editorial paper collage and screen-print, cobalt blue, coral, saffron, mint, warm ivory, charcoal, tactile grain, high contrast, simple enough for mobile, empathetic and non-corporate.
```

## `evidence-lens.webp`

```text
Create a wide 16:9 editorial illustration about reading artist success stories critically. A bright spotlight reveals one celebrated fictional musician and a polished staircase, while a researcher rotates a large transparent prism so the wider scene becomes visible: many hidden working musicians, different starting resources, teams, timing, health, obligations, failed experiments, and alternate satisfying destinations. The spotlighted artist is not villainized; the image simply reveals missing context and survivor bias. No text, numbers, labels, logos, real people, recognizable awards, or fake data. Sophisticated screen-print and layered paper collage, cobalt, coral, saffron, mint, ivory, charcoal, tactile grain, strong visual hierarchy, generous negative space, humane and thoughtful.
```
