# 음악 마케팅 + 아티스트 브랜딩 시각 가이드 이미지 프롬프트

모든 이미지는 OpenAI `imagegen`으로 생성했다. 그림은 개념 이해를 위한 생성 모식도이며 실제 아티스트, 캠페인 화면, 플랫폼 UI, 성과 그래프 또는 연구 데이터를 재현하지 않는다. 이미지 안에 글자·숫자·로고를 넣지 않았다.

## `hero.webp`

```text
Create a wide 16:9 editorial illustration for a Korean beginner-friendly research guide about music marketing plus artist branding. Show an entirely fictional music artist at the center of a vivid coherent world built from their music: sound waves become recurring colors, stage shapes, album objects, clothing textures, live moments, and fan-made responses. Around the world is a humane pathway where unfamiliar people discover a fragment, listen more deeply, recognize the artist's promise, join a community, attend or buy, then return and share—without looking like a sales funnel or corporate diagram. Keep strong dark negative space on the left for an HTML headline overlay. Contemporary risograph, editorial collage and hand-painted texture, midnight plum, acid green, hot pink, sky blue, tangerine, warm cream, high contrast, emotionally rich. No text, numbers, labels, logos, brand marks, platform UI, celebrity likenesses, fake charts, or generic business people.
```
